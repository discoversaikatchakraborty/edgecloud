Superseded EdgeCloud rerun outputs, moved here 8 October 2026
=============================================================

Why these are here, and what replaces them.

THE DEFECT

The package's own seed list is CONFIG["seeds"] = [101, 202, 303, 404, 505], in
upstream/prototype_v2.3/config.json. Every published real-data number in the manuscript is a
mean over those five seeds; the retained package output results/real_data_summary.csv
confirms it, for example cloud_transmit_all under normal connectivity at 435.8 errors, which
is exactly the value Table XIII prints.

Two rerun protocols were frozen with seeds [0, 1, 2, 3, 4] instead:

  itemC_protocol.json  ->  itemC_protocol_rev1.json   (archived here)
  itemD_protocol.json  ->  itemD_protocol_rev1.json   (copied here)

Item C's own reproduction gate caught it. Its instruction is that the current, unhandled
column must reproduce the manuscript's Table XIII before the handled column is interpreted.
On the wrong seeds it did not, on six of the ten methods:

  method                       manuscript   wrong-seed run
  Image only                        420.0            420.0   agrees
  Transmit all                      435.8            435.4
  Fixed rule                        464.2            465.0
  Confidence gate                   437.0            437.0   agrees
  Random within budget              462.0            458.2
  Greedy by uncertainty             466.8            466.8   agrees
  Greedy, proposed utility          447.0            447.2
  Proposed (exact knapsack)         447.0            447.2
  Fixed compression                 483.0            482.8
  Text only                         494.0            494.0   agrees

The gap is small on the deterministic arms and largest, 3.8 errors, on the randomised
baseline, which is what a wrong seed list predicts. No conclusion was drawn from the handled
column, because the protocol forbids interpreting it until the current column reproduces.

WHAT IS AFFECTED, AND WHAT IS NOT

  item A   NOT affected. It uses seeds [0..4] as well, but its cells are scored against a
           majority baseline computed on the same records, never against a published value,
           so reproducing a printed table is not part of its decision rule. Its seed basis
           is now stated explicitly in its own record rather than left implicit.
  item C   AFFECTED. Re-frozen as itemC_protocol_rev2.json on the package seeds and rerun.
  item D   AFFECTED. Re-frozen as itemD_protocol_rev2.json on the package seeds and rerun.
           The rev1 files are COPIED here rather than moved, so the committed state stays
           intact until the rev2 outputs exist.
  item E   NOT affected. Its twenty seeds begin with the package's five and extend them,
           which is what Rerun_Spec item E asks for.

NOTHING HERE IS DELETED. A failed or superseded run stays visible; that is the protocol's
rule and the reason this directory exists rather than a removal. These files are the evidence
for the defect described above and must not be cited as results.
